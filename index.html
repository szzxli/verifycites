Free tools that check citations the way you actually use them: does it exist, do its details match the record, and — for papers — does its abstract support your claim?

A citation that exists does not by itself prove it supports your argument. Existence, metadata match, and claim support are shown separately.

Papers are checked live in your browser. A legal brief in the browser is extracted into a checklist: every row is NOT_CHECKED. Existence and details match are not checked there.

Extraction happens in your browser; the only thing that ever leaves it is a citation identifier, sent to a public database for lookup.
