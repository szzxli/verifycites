iImportant limits — please read

Not legal advice. This tool only checks whether a cited case exists in CourtListener and whether the case name matches. It does not check whether the case supports your argument, whether quotes or pin cites are accurate, or whether the case is still good law. A case marked NOT FOUND may still exist (CourtListener's coverage is incomplete); a case marked VERIFIED may still be wrong for your purpose. Read every case you cite.

06 Optional guideImmigration citations

An immigration brief mixes federal court cases, BIA precedent (Matter of), INA provisions, and 8 CFR provisions. This page classifies each extracted row and names its source line; immigration rows are not yet looked up live.

Not yet covered: no live BIA, INA, or 8 CFR lookup in this page; no judgment on whether a case supports your argument or is still good law. A BIA precedent can be overruled, and circuit precedent controls; some Title 8 provisions may be enjoined, so eCFR text alone never proves a provision is currently in effect. The core statute and regulation text (INA, 8 CFR) changes rarely; what changes more often is policy guidance and new BIA precedent.

Memo-currency rule: a memo's currency is shown only when the official source itself states supersession, rescission, or replacement — never inferred from list presence or absence.

Check by hand at the official sources:

  • EOIR Agency Decisions (BIA precedent volumes)
  • EOIR Virtual Law Library (recent decisions)
  • USCIS AAO non-precedent decisions search
  • USCIS Policy Manual
  • eCFR Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality)
  • OLRC Title 8 download (U.S. Code bulk XML)
